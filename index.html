CS201HardData Structures

Prove that the height of an AVL tree with n nodes is strictly bounded by 1.44 log₂(n + 2) - 0.328.

Topic: Balanced Binary Trees, Fibonacci bounds, mathematical induction recurrence proofs.
Hint: Let N(h) be the minimum number of nodes in an AVL tree of height h. Show N(h) = N(h−1) + N(h−2) + 1, relate it to Fibonacci numbers, then take logarithms.
MATH302MediumLinear Algebra

Under what condition is a symmetric matrix A strictly positive-definite?

Topic: Sylvester’s Criterion, Eigenvalue positivity, quadratic forms xᵀAx > 0.
Hint: A is positive-definite exactly when all its eigenvalues are positive, equivalently when every leading principal minor is positive (Sylvester's criterion).
PHYS105HardElectromagnetism

Derive the displacement current term in Ampère-Maxwell law for a discharging parallel-plate capacitor.

Topic: Continuity equation, dielectric boundary flux, magnetic circulation curl(B).
Hint: Start from the continuity equation, substitute ∇·E = ρ/ε₀, and show that adding ε₀ ∂E/∂t to the current density keeps Ampère's law consistent.
